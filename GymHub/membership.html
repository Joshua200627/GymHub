Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our memberships

Is there a joining fee?

Yes, there is a one-time joining fee of $49 for all new members. However, we often run promotions where this fee is waived.

Can I cancel my membership at any time?

All our memberships require a minimum 3-month commitment. After that, you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Do you offer family memberships?

Yes, we offer family memberships with discounted rates for additional family members. Please contact us for more information.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, you can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year for medical reasons or extended travel.

Are there any age restrictions?

Members must be at least 16 years old. Members aged 16-17 require parental consent and must be accompanied by an adult during their workouts.
